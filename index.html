Mid-November is shoulder season: many entrances and high roads are closed, services are thin, and weather turns fast. We only publish pages for entrances that are open; closures are listed on each park page.

How ParkReady works

Updated weekly. Sourced, not guessed.

Each week we pull official alerts and operating hours from the NPS Data API, read each park's conditions, roads and news pages, and grab National Weather Service point forecasts for every entrance. Pages show a "Week of" label and list every source, so you can check anything yourself.

We don't publish entrance wait times, bear sightings, or incident counts we can't source. If NPS hasn't said it, neither do we. Conditions change hourly in winter, so check the linked NPS page or call the park's road line the morning you go.

Follow official NPS rules, posted closures and ranger instructions. ParkReady Guides is not affiliated with the National Park Service.
