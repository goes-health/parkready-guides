> ParkReady Guides by GOES Health · Week of Nov 9–15, 2026. Weekly entrance briefings for 6 of America's busiest national parks (17 open entrances): which entrances are open, this week's official closures and weather, and what to prepare for at that entrance and season. Mid-November is shoulder season: many entrances and high roads are closed, services are thin, and weather turns fast. We only publish pages for entrances that are open; closures are listed on each park page.

## How ParkReady works

Each week we pull official alerts and operating hours from the NPS Data API, read each park's conditions, roads and news pages, and grab National Weather Service point forecasts for every entrance. We don't publish entrance wait times, wildlife sightings, or incident counts we can't source. If NPS hasn't said it, neither do we.
