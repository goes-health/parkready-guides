Glacier National Park this week

Going-to-the-Sun Road closed at Avalanche Creek and Jackson Glacier Overlook for the season.

Glacier is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, but it's in its late-fall, low-service season. Going-to-the-Sun Road is closed for the season at Avalanche Creek on the west side and at Jackson Glacier Overlook on the east. The lower road from West Glacier to Lake McDonald Lodge is plowed year-round, weather permitting. There's no fuel inside the park and no in-park lodging this time of year. Entrance fees still apply; use the self-pay stations when entrances aren't staffed. NPS alertNPSNPSNPS API

Know the risks

What matters in Glacier right now

Teach, don't scare: these are the hazards NPS emphasizes this season, with what to do.

Grizzly and black bears

Glacier has habitat for nearly 1,000 bears of both species. Stay 100 yards from bears and wolves (25 yards from other wildlife), carry bear spray, make noise and hike in groups: Glacier has had no reported attacks on groups of four or more. In fall, bears forage hard before denning. NPSNPSNPS

In GOESWildlife Encounters: location-specific risk indicators for bears and other dangerous animals

Cold water

Water is the number one cause of fatalities in Glacier. Use extreme caution near lakes and streams. NPS

In GOESEnvironmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments

Tularemia

NPS confirmed tularemia in dead snowshoe hares in Many Glacier (Oct 15 release) and the St. Mary Lake area (Sept 17 release). Use insect repellent, check for ticks, don't touch carcasses, drink only treated or boiled water, and see a doctor if you get fever, chills or sores within two weeks of your visit. NPS newsNPS news

Winter self-sufficiency

Most businesses and services in and near the park are closed from early October to late May. Expect cold, blustery days and be entirely self-sufficient. NPSNPS
