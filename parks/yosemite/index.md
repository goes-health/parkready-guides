# Yosemite National Park this week

> Week of Nov 16–22, 2026 · CA · Tioga and Glacier Point roads are closed for snow; a winter storm watch runs to Nov 20. Facts fetched Nov 16, 2026 from official NPS and NWS sources.

HTML version: https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/

Yosemite is open 24 hours a day, but the high country is shutting down. Tioga Road (Hwy 120 through the park) and Glacier Point Road are temporarily closed for snow, and NWS has a Winter Storm Watch for the park area until Nov 20. Wawona Road (Hwy 41), El Portal Road (Hwy 140) and Big Oak Flat Road (Hwy 120 west) are open. Carry tire chains: they can become mandatory at any time, even for 4WD. Sources: [NPS Yosemite alerts (NPS Data API, fetched Nov 16)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [NWS Hanford: Winter Storm Watch (issued Nov 16, until Nov 20 4 am PST) and Winter Weather Advisory (until Nov 17 4 am PST)](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=37.507&lon=-119.632)

## Entrances open this week

- [Arch Rock Entrance](https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/arch-rock-entrance.md) (El Portal / Mariposa (Hwy 140)): **Open — Hwy 140 / El Portal Road**. Arch Rock is the Highway 140 entrance from Merced, Mariposa and El Portal, along the Merced River. NPS puts it about 20–25 minutes from Yosemite Valley.
- [Big Oak Flat Entrance](https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/big-oak-flat-entrance.md) (Groveland (Hwy 120 west)): **Open — weekday delays**. Big Oak Flat is the Highway 120 entrance from the Bay Area via Groveland, next to Hodgdon Meadow. With Tioga Road closed, Hwy 120 inside the park now leads only toward Crane Flat and Yosemite Valley (about 45 minutes away).
- [South Entrance](https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/south-entrance.md) (Fish Camp / Oakhurst (Hwy 41)): **Open — Winter Storm Watch to Nov 20**. The South Entrance on Highway 41 from Fresno, Oakhurst and Fish Camp leads to the Mariposa Grove of Giant Sequoias, Wawona, and then Yosemite Valley, a little over an hour north.
- [Hetch Hetchy Entrance](https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/hetch-hetchy-entrance.md) (Evergreen Road (off Hwy 120)): **Daylight only — closes for snow**. Hetch Hetchy is Yosemite's quiet northwest corner, a reservoir valley at about 3,900 feet reached on Evergreen and Hetch Hetchy roads off Hwy 120. NPS puts it about 1.5 hours from Yosemite Valley.

## What matters in Yosemite right now

- **Black bears in all areas.** NPS: bears remain active in all areas of the park. Store food properly, use bear-proof dumpsters, and stay at least 50 yards from bears in forest and meadows — but yell at and scare away a bear that approaches you or is in a developed area. Yosemite has an estimated 300–500 black bears and no grizzlies. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Yosemite bears](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/bears.htm)
  - In GOES: Wildlife Encounters: location-specific risk indicators for bears and other dangerous animals
- **Rivers and slick rock.** Waterfalls are flowing low, as usual for November, but NPS still asks visitors to use caution along rivers and creeks: rocks are slippery even when dry. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
  - In GOES: Offline interactive health assessment + 50+ visual how-to guides
- **Winter storm, chains and ice.** NWS has a Winter Storm Watch for the park area until Nov 20 at 4 am PST (plus a Winter Weather Advisory near Wawona until Nov 17), with rain and snow on and off from Monday through Thursday and highs mostly in the 30s and 40s. Carry chains even with 4WD and expect icy spots on mostly dry roads. Sources: [NWS Hanford: Winter Storm Watch (issued Nov 16, until Nov 20 4 am PST) and Winter Weather Advisory (until Nov 17 4 am PST)](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=37.507&lon=-119.632); [NWS Sacramento: Winter Storm Watch (issued Nov 15, until Nov 20 4 am PST)](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=37.8&lon=-119.874); [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [National Weather Service point forecasts (api.weather.gov, fetched Nov 16)](https://www.weather.gov/)
  - In GOES: Environmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments
- **Smoke and air quality.** The Dome Fire (Sept–Oct) was 86% contained with minimal activity as of NPS's Oct 13 update, and fire closures remain around the Glacier Point Road area. Air quality can still be affected by fires elsewhere or prescribed burns, so check current AQI before strenuous hikes. Sources: [Dome Fire update, Oct 13, 2026](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/news/dome-fire-update-october-13-2026-morning.htm); [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [AirNow fire & smoke map](https://fire.airnow.gov/)
  - In GOES: Respiratory Hazards: smoke, pollution and pollen (AQI) monitoring
