# Yosemite National Park this week

> Week of Nov 23–29, 2026 · CA · Tioga and Glacier Point roads are closed for the season; the valley is mild and mostly dry this week. Facts fetched Nov 23, 2026 from official NPS and NWS sources.

HTML version: https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/

Yosemite is open 24 hours a day, and the high country is now closed for winter: Tioga Road (Hwy 120 through the park) and Glacier Point Road are closed for the season and usually reopen in late May or June. El Portal Road (Hwy 140) is open; for Wawona Road (Hwy 41) and Big Oak Flat Road (Hwy 120 west), NPS asks you to call 209/372-0200 (press 1, then 1) for current status. Last week's winter storm watch has ended, and NWS forecasts a mostly sunny week with a chance of light rain Tuesday night into Wednesday. Carry tire chains: they can become mandatory at any time, even for 4WD. Sources: [NPS Yosemite alerts (NPS Data API, fetched Nov 23)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 20, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [National Weather Service point forecasts (api.weather.gov, fetched Nov 23)](https://www.weather.gov/)

## Entrances open this week

- [Arch Rock Entrance](https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/arch-rock-entrance.md) (El Portal / Mariposa (Hwy 140)): **Open — Hwy 140 / El Portal Road**. Arch Rock is the Highway 140 entrance from Merced, Mariposa and El Portal, along the Merced River. NPS puts it about 20–25 minutes from Yosemite Valley.
- [Big Oak Flat Entrance](https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/big-oak-flat-entrance.md) (Groveland (Hwy 120 west)): **Open — weekday delays**. Big Oak Flat is the Highway 120 entrance from the Bay Area via Groveland, next to Hodgdon Meadow. With Tioga Road closed, Hwy 120 inside the park now leads only toward Crane Flat and Yosemite Valley (about 45 minutes away).
- [South Entrance](https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/south-entrance.md) (Fish Camp / Oakhurst (Hwy 41)): **Call for Hwy 41 status**. The South Entrance on Highway 41 from Fresno, Oakhurst and Fish Camp leads to the Mariposa Grove of Giant Sequoias, Wawona, and then Yosemite Valley, a little over an hour north.
- [Hetch Hetchy Entrance](https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/hetch-hetchy-entrance.md) (Evergreen Road (off Hwy 120)): **Daylight only — closes for snow**. Hetch Hetchy is Yosemite's quiet northwest corner, a reservoir valley at about 3,900 feet reached on Evergreen and Hetch Hetchy roads off Hwy 120. NPS puts it about 1.5 hours from Yosemite Valley.

## What matters in Yosemite right now

- **Black bears in all areas.** NPS: bears remain active in all areas of the park. Store food properly, use bear-proof dumpsters, and stay at least 50 yards from bears in forest and meadows — but yell at and scare away a bear that approaches you or is in a developed area. Yosemite has an estimated 300–500 black bears and no grizzlies. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 20, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Yosemite bears](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/bears.htm)
  - In GOES: Wildlife Encounters: location-specific risk indicators for bears and other dangerous animals
- **Rivers and slick rock.** Waterfalls are flowing low, as usual for November, but NPS still asks visitors to use caution along rivers and creeks: rocks are slippery even when dry. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 20, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
  - In GOES: Offline interactive health assessment + 50+ visual how-to guides
- **Frosty mornings, chains and ice.** There are no NWS watches or warnings for the park this week. Days are mild (50s to mid-60s in the Valley), but NWS forecasts widespread frost near Big Oak Flat and Hetch Hetchy with lows around 28–34°F. NPS: carry chains even with 4WD and expect icy spots on mostly dry roads. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 20, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [National Weather Service point forecasts (api.weather.gov, fetched Nov 23)](https://www.weather.gov/)
  - In GOES: Environmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments
- **Smoke and air quality.** The Dome Fire (Sept–Oct) was 86% contained with minimal activity as of NPS's Oct 13 update, and fire closures remain around the Glacier Point Road area. Air quality can still be affected by fires elsewhere or prescribed burns, so check current AQI before strenuous hikes. Sources: [Dome Fire update, Oct 13, 2026](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/news/dome-fire-update-october-13-2026-morning.htm); [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 20, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [AirNow fire & smoke map](https://fire.airnow.gov/)
  - In GOES: Respiratory Hazards: smoke, pollution and pollen (AQI) monitoring
