# Yosemite Big Oak Flat Entrance: this week

> Week of Nov 16–22, 2026 · Yosemite National Park · Groveland (Hwy 120 west) · Status: **Open — weekday delays**. Facts fetched Nov 16, 2026 from official NPS and NWS sources.

HTML version: https://parks.goes.health/parks/yosemite/big-oak-flat-entrance.html

Big Oak Flat is the Highway 120 entrance from the Bay Area via Groveland, next to Hodgdon Meadow. With Tioga Road closed, Hwy 120 inside the park now leads only toward Crane Flat and Yosemite Valley (about 45 minutes away).

## This week at the Big Oak Flat Entrance (official conditions)

- **Heads-up:** Big Oak Flat Road: open, with 15-minute delays on weekdays for fire-safety vegetation work between Foresta and Big Oak Flat. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
- **Closed:** Tioga Road (east of Crane Flat) is temporarily closed for snow. Sources: [NPS Yosemite alerts (NPS Data API, fetched Nov 16)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
- **Heads-up:** NWS Winter Storm Watch for this area, issued Nov 15, in effect until Nov 20 at 4 am PST. Sources: [NWS Sacramento: Winter Storm Watch (issued Nov 15, until Nov 20 4 am PST)](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=37.8&lon=-119.874)
- **Info:** Hodgdon Meadow Campground (by this entrance): open, first-come, first-served. Crane Flat, Tamarack Flat and White Wolf campgrounds are closed. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
- **Info:** Fuel: Crane Flat, 24/7 by credit card. Sources: [Yosemite driving directions, fuel & GPS warning](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/driving.htm)

**Entrance fee:** Private vehicle $35. Non-US residents 16+ pay an additional $100 nonresident fee unless entering with an annual / America the Beautiful pass. Sources: [NPS Yosemite fees & passes](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/fees.htm); [NPS Data API (parks, operating hours, fees)](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/developer/api-documentation.htm)

## What actually matters here this season

The goal is a good trip, not a scary one. These are the hazards NPS highlights for this area and season, and what to do about each.

- **Freezing nights and wet snow.** This week's NWS forecast here: rain Monday, nights around 15–24°F, and rain and snow again Wednesday and Thursday under a Winter Storm Watch. Being wet in freezing weather is how hikers get hypothermia. Sources: [National Weather Service point forecasts (api.weather.gov, fetched Nov 16)](https://www.weather.gov/)
  - In GOES: Environmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments
- **Bears at campgrounds and pullouts.** Hodgdon Meadow is open. Use the food lockers, keep a clean camp, and stay 50 yards from bears in the forest. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Yosemite bears](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/bears.htm)
  - In GOES: Wildlife Encounters: location-specific risk indicators for bears and other dangerous animals
- **Road work and wildlife on the road.** Expect weekday work zones on Big Oak Flat Road. NPS posts signs where bears have been hit by vehicles, so drive the speed limit. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Yosemite bears](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/bears.htm)
  - In GOES: Wildlife Encounters: location-specific risk indicators for dangerous animals

## Yosemite park-wide

- **Black bears in all areas.** NPS: bears remain active in all areas of the park. Store food properly, use bear-proof dumpsters, and stay at least 50 yards from bears in forest and meadows — but yell at and scare away a bear that approaches you or is in a developed area. Yosemite has an estimated 300–500 black bears and no grizzlies. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Yosemite bears](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/bears.htm)
  - In GOES: Wildlife Encounters: location-specific risk indicators for bears and other dangerous animals
- **Rivers and slick rock.** Waterfalls are flowing low, as usual for November, but NPS still asks visitors to use caution along rivers and creeks: rocks are slippery even when dry. Sources: [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
  - In GOES: Offline interactive health assessment + 50+ visual how-to guides
- **Winter storm, chains and ice.** NWS has a Winter Storm Watch for the park area until Nov 20 at 4 am PST (plus a Winter Weather Advisory near Wawona until Nov 17), with rain and snow on and off from Monday through Thursday and highs mostly in the 30s and 40s. Carry chains even with 4WD and expect icy spots on mostly dry roads. Sources: [NWS Hanford: Winter Storm Watch (issued Nov 16, until Nov 20 4 am PST) and Winter Weather Advisory (until Nov 17 4 am PST)](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=37.507&lon=-119.632); [NWS Sacramento: Winter Storm Watch (issued Nov 15, until Nov 20 4 am PST)](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=37.8&lon=-119.874); [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [National Weather Service point forecasts (api.weather.gov, fetched Nov 16)](https://www.weather.gov/)
  - In GOES: Environmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments
- **Smoke and air quality.** The Dome Fire (Sept–Oct) was 86% contained with minimal activity as of NPS's Oct 13 update, and fire closures remain around the Glacier Point Road area. Air quality can still be affected by fires elsewhere or prescribed burns, so check current AQI before strenuous hikes. Sources: [Dome Fire update, Oct 13, 2026](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/news/dome-fire-update-october-13-2026-morning.htm); [Yosemite Current Conditions (updated Nov 13, 2026)](https://www.nps.gov/yose/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [AirNow fire & smoke map](https://fire.airnow.gov/)
  - In GOES: Respiratory Hazards: smoke, pollution and pollen (AQI) monitoring
