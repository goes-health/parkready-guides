Zion Kolob Canyons Entrance: this week

Open — may close for snow or ice Week of Nov 9–15, 2026

Kolob Canyons is Zion's quieter northwest district, at I-15 Exit 40, 17 miles south of Cedar City and about an hour from Zion Canyon. A five-mile scenic drive leads to viewpoints and wilderness trails.

Park-wide

Zion risks everywhere in the park

Flash floods

Flash floods can happen at any time in Zion, carrying trees and boulders. The Narrows closes when flow exceeds 150 cfs or when NWS issues a Flash Flood Warning. Rain and thunderstorms are forecast Friday night into Saturday this week. NPSNPSNWS

In GOES125+ extreme weather alerts

Toxic cyanobacteria

Don't drink water from rivers, streams or pools, and don't submerge your head in them. Use potable water, and treat spring water only as a last resort. NPSNPS alert

In GOESOffline interactive health assessment + 50+ visual how-to guides

Hypothermia in the canyons

NPS: hypothermia is a major hazard in narrow canyons, and immersion in water is the fastest way to lose body heat. Avoid cotton and eat before you get chilled. NPS

In GOESEnvironmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments

Cliffs

Falls from cliffs on trails have caused deaths. Loose sand on stone is slippery, so stay on the trail and back from edges. NPS
