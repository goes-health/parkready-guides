# Zion South Entrance: this week

> Week of Nov 9–15, 2026 · Zion National Park · Springdale, UT (SR-9) · Status: **Open — shuttle through Nov 28**. Facts fetched Nov 12, 2026 from official NPS and NWS sources.

HTML version: https://parks.goes.health/parks/zion/south-entrance.html

The South Entrance at Springdale leads to the Zion Canyon Visitor Center, where shuttles leave for the Zion Canyon Scenic Drive, the Riverside Walk and The Narrows.

## This week at the South Entrance (official conditions)

- **Open:** Zion Canyon shuttle, Oct 25 – Nov 28: first bus 7 am from the Visitor Center, last bus up-canyon 5 pm, last bus out of Temple of Sinawava 6:15 pm. No service Nov 29 – Dec 25. Springdale Line: 8 am – 6 pm through Nov 28. Sources: [Zion Canyon shuttle schedule](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/zion-canyon-shuttle-system.htm)
- **Info:** Zion Canyon Visitor Center, park store and wilderness desk: 8 am – 5 pm. Human History Museum: 10 am – 5 pm. Sources: [Zion operating hours & facility hours](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/hours.htm)
- **Closed:** Watchman Trail is closed pending repairs and Hidden Canyon is closed (rockfall). Angels Landing requires a permit. Sources: [NPS Zion alerts (NPS Data API, fetched Nov 12)](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Zion Current Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Angels Landing permits](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/angels-landing-hiking-permits.htm)
- **Heads-up:** Parking is limited. When park lots fill, park in Springdale; vehicles outside designated spaces can be cited and towed. Sources: [NPS Zion alerts (NPS Data API, fetched Nov 12)](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
- **Info:** Watchman Campground is open year-round (reservations required). South Campground closes seasonally in winter. Sources: [Zion Current Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [Zion operating hours & facility hours](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/hours.htm)

**Entrance fee:** Private vehicle $35. Non-US residents 16+ pay an additional $100 nonresident fee unless entering with an annual / America the Beautiful pass. Angels Landing requires a separate permit. Sources: [NPS Zion fees & passes](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/fees.htm); [NPS Data API (parks, operating hours, fees)](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/developer/api-documentation.htm); [Angels Landing permits](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/angels-landing-hiking-permits.htm)

## What actually matters here this season

The goal is a good trip, not a scary one. These are the hazards NPS highlights for this area and season, and what to do about each.

- **The Narrows in November.** Cold water and short days. Check USGS flow and the NWS forecast the morning you go; the route closes at 150 cfs or under a Flash Flood Warning. Showers and thunderstorms are forecast Friday night and Saturday. Sources: [Zion Current Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [National Weather Service point forecasts (api.weather.gov, fetched Nov 12)](https://www.weather.gov/)
  - In GOES: Environmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments
- **Don't miss the last shuttle.** NPS says not to wait for the last shuttle of the day. Being stuck in the canyon after dark in November means a long, cold walk out. Sources: [Zion Canyon shuttle schedule](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/zion-canyon-shuttle-system.htm)
  - In GOES: Environmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments
- **Cyanobacteria in the Virgin River.** Don't drink river water or put your head under. NPS's advisory covers rivers, streams and pools parkwide. Sources: [Zion toxic cyanobacteria advisory](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/toxic-cyanobacteria-bloom-in-the-virgin-river-and-the-streams-of-zion-national-park.htm)
  - In GOES: Offline interactive health assessment + 50+ visual how-to guides

## Zion park-wide

- **Flash floods.** Flash floods can happen at any time in Zion, carrying trees and boulders. The Narrows closes when flow exceeds 150 cfs or when NWS issues a Flash Flood Warning. Rain and thunderstorms are forecast Friday night into Saturday this week. Sources: [Zion safety: flash floods, cliffs, hypothermia, water](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/safety.htm); [Zion Current Conditions](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/conditions.htm); [National Weather Service point forecasts (api.weather.gov, fetched Nov 12)](https://www.weather.gov/)
  - In GOES: 125+ extreme weather alerts
- **Toxic cyanobacteria.** Don't drink water from rivers, streams or pools, and don't submerge your head in them. Use potable water, and treat spring water only as a last resort. Sources: [Zion toxic cyanobacteria advisory](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/toxic-cyanobacteria-bloom-in-the-virgin-river-and-the-streams-of-zion-national-park.htm); [NPS Zion alerts (NPS Data API, fetched Nov 12)](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/conditions.htm)
  - In GOES: Offline interactive health assessment + 50+ visual how-to guides
- **Hypothermia in the canyons.** NPS: hypothermia is a major hazard in narrow canyons, and immersion in water is the fastest way to lose body heat. Avoid cotton and eat before you get chilled. Sources: [Zion safety: flash floods, cliffs, hypothermia, water](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/safety.htm)
  - In GOES: Environmental Health: hypothermia and frostbite assessments
- **Cliffs.** Falls from cliffs on trails have caused deaths. Loose sand on stone is slippery, so stay on the trail and back from edges. Sources: [Zion safety: flash floods, cliffs, hypothermia, water](https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/safety.htm)
  - In GOES: Offline interactive health assessment + 50+ visual how-to guides
